encrypt-resume offline · client-side

Paste your resume (Markdown or plain text), choose a passphrase, and download an encrypted JSON blob to commit at assets/resume.enc.json. Encryption runs entirely in your browser using the Web Crypto API — your plaintext never leaves your machine.
